#define SWEEP_PASSES    2
#define SWEEP_PASS_MS   800
#define SWEEP_PEAK      235
#define SWEEP_OVERLAP   320

Reading it: OVERLAP 256 = neighbours cross at ~50% (clean hand-off); >256 = softer, more of the row lit at once; <256 = a dark gap between LEDs (sequential blink, not a crossfade). PASS_MS is one L→R wipe; PASSES wipes then a short pause, mimicking the tell re-firing each ~1 s poll while in sun. Brightness here tracks the pre-gamma perceptual value, which is what the eye sees on the real card after the firmware's gamma² correction.
